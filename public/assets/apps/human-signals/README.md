# Human Signals identity

Original deterministic vector mark by Scaling Human. A cyan ring and a gold
wearable loop share one signal trace. Device shapes remain distinct, matching
the app's policy of keeping provenance and baselines separate. No vendor marks
or letters are used. This is the app identity, not a replacement for the Scaling
Human organization logo.

icon.svg is the editable full-bleed iOS source. icon-1024.png is the app master.
Additional PNG sizes are supplied for website and future resource use. Grayscale
is provided separately; mark-mono.svg is a white transparent mark for dark surfaces.
No raster image-generation tool or source video was used.
